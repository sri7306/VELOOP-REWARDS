Frequently asked questions

How do I earn Gems?

Open Earn Gems and choose an activity. The server checks whether the activity can be claimed and records successful claims.

How do I convert Gems?

Open Exchange Center, enter an amount, preview the rate, and confirm. Your balances update after the server completes the conversion.

What are VEs?

VEs are the second reward unit in this application. They can be used to redeem available rewards in the marketplace.

How do I redeem rewards?

Choose a reward from the marketplace and confirm. You need enough VEs and the reward must be available.

How do I change my password?

Open Security and submit your current password and a valid new password.

Contact support

Submit a ticket. This local application records tickets in its database; it does not send email automatically.
